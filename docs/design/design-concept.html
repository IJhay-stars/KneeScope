03Intended use

Research and teaching review of knee MRI studies that the team is permitted to use. Lets a reviewer compare model scores with dataset labels and inspect limitations.

04Out of scope

Diagnosis, triage, treatment decisions, use on studies outside the permitted dataset, and any claim about clinical benefit or cross-site generalisation.

05Known limitations

  1. Scores are uncalibrated. A score of 0.85 is not an 85% chance of injury.
  2. ROC AUC measures ranking, not accuracy. An AUC of 0.80 is not “80% correct”.
  3. Model attention and influential slices show what affected the score; they do not localise an injury.
  4. A model trained on one dataset is not validated on another. Single-site evaluation does not establish generalisation across sites or scanners.
  5. Separate MRI planes are independent acquisitions and are not spatially synchronised in the viewer.
